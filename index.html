Bahá’ís in Iran: a crackdown that is widening

Since 1979, Iran’s largest non-Muslim religious minority has faced a state policy designed to eliminate it as a viable community in the land of its birth. Since the January 2026 protests and the February outbreak of conflict, that persecution has intensified on every front.

750+persecutory acts documented, June to November 2025
200+raids on Bahá’í homes and businesses in that period
110+Bahá’ís arrested in that period
